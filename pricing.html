When will pricing be announced?

Once it has been validated with the founding cohort. Registered organisations hear first, with founding terms that outlast the early-access period.

Will there be a free trial?

Yes. It's being built now and will be announced to registered organisations first. Until it exists, we won't put a trial button on this site.

Can a single person really use Vesta?

Yes, deliberately. The Individual tier exists because governance discipline shouldn't require an enterprise agreement. Start alone; the Team and Enterprise tiers are there when your portfolio grows.

What do founding customers actually get?

Early access, priority onboarding, a real say in the roadmap and first sight of pricing. In return, we get the thing we value most: a portfolio to build around.
