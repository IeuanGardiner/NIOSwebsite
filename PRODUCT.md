# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

- **Primary buyer:** PMO Director / Head of Portfolio at a large organisation. Owns governance, reports to boards, carries the risk exposure. Visits on office Wi-Fi, sceptical of vendor gloss, allergic to enterprise-PPM heaviness. The site must earn their trust in 10 seconds.
- **Champion:** the day-to-day programme/project manager reconciling a dozen RAID spreadsheets. The site should make them think "this would make my Tuesday easier."
- **Deliberately reachable downward:** individuals and small businesses. Enterprise-grade governance that doesn't need an enterprise to run it.

## Product Purpose

Vesta is a portfolio risk and change management platform for PMOs: risks, issues, changes, projects, programmes and reports, live, permissioned and audit-ready. It replaces governance-by-spreadsheet (RAID spreadsheets in SharePoint, approvals over email, stale steering packs). The wedge: more rigour than Excel, a fraction of the weight of enterprise PPM (Planview, ServiceNow SPM). The site's job: make a stranger conclude within 10 seconds that this is a real, credible SaaS product and register interest.

## Brand Personality

Composed, rigorous, modern. Speaks like a good governance lead: precise, calm, never breathless. Confidence comes from showing the real product (dark-mode screenshots) and telling the truth (early access, no invented proof). Target calibre: Linear / Stripe.

## Anti-references

- The corporate-brochure look of legacy PPM vendors (Planview-era stock photos, feature-matrix walls).
- AI-landing-page slop: gradient text, glassmorphism-by-default, identical icon-card grids, eyebrow labels over every section, fabricated logos/testimonials/stats.
- Heavy WebGL scenes, autoplaying video, scroll-jacking. Performance is a trust signal.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the product, not an illustration of it.** Real screenshots in slim browser chrome are the credibility engine.
2. **Honesty is the brand.** No invented testimonials, stats or logos; placeholders are labelled as placeholders. Founding-cohort framing carries the social proof until real quotes exist.
3. **Motion as seasoning, never the meal.** Subtle drift, settle-on-scroll, micro-interactions; everything collapses under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
4. **Fast on office Wi-Fi.** Lean pages, lazy-loaded below-fold imagery, no new runtime dependencies.
5. **Excellent in both themes.** Dark, confident openings on every page; light/dark rhythm through the body; AA+ contrast everywhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA minimum: 4.5:1 body contrast in both themes, visible focus states, skip link, semantic landmarks and heading order, keyboard-complete navigation (focus-trapped mobile menu), `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses all motion to static, responsive from 360px to ultrawide. UK English (`lang="en-GB"`).
